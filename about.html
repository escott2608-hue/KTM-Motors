The Origin Story

From Assembly Bottlenecks to Automated Inventory Systems

KTM Systems was founded during an intensive engineering project aimed at solving a critical issue in manufacturing: multi-facility inventory misalignments. While witnessing high-speed assembly lines ground to a halt due to missing parts, co-founders Evan, Graham, and Shane recognized that traditional spreadsheets and fragmented legacy software were failing modern industrial plants.

Combining their expertise in relational database architecture, systems design, and operational management, the trio set out to engineer a centralized inventory backbone. Together, they created a unified platform capable of connecting warehouse floor logging directly with executive reorder management and real-time stock thresholds across North America.
